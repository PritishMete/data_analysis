Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how InsightFlow handles information when you use the InsightFlow application and related services. It should be read together with our Terms and Conditions.

Privacy-first analytics: InsightFlow is designed to help users analyze data while limiting unnecessary collection and disclosure of workbook contents. The exact processing available to you depends on the features you use and your organization's configuration.

1. Information we may process

  • Account and profile information: email address, display name, authentication identifiers, and profile or organization details you provide.
  • Organization and access information: workspace, branch, role, invitations, and authorization records needed to provide team features and enforce access permissions.
  • Analytics inputs and outputs: files, tables, queries, results, and reports you choose to submit or create. Some analysis workflows are designed to process workbook data locally in your browser or application. Check the description of the specific feature you use before uploading or sharing data.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: information needed to operate, protect, troubleshoot, and improve the service, such as error details and service activity.
  • Optional Gmail integration: if an authorized user connects a Google account, the service may receive the account identity and OAuth permissions shown on Google's consent screen. The integration is intended to send InsightFlow invitation emails and support the connected sender configuration.

2. How we use information

Information is used to authenticate users, maintain accounts and workspaces, enforce role-based access, deliver invitations requested by authorized users, provide analytics features, protect the service, troubleshoot errors, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information.

3. Workbook and business data

Do not submit information unless you are authorized to use it. InsightFlow's local-analysis workflows are designed to keep workbook rows and values in the local execution environment by default. Other features may process information on service infrastructure when needed to provide the feature. Organization administrators should configure access and data handling to match their obligations, and users should review feature-specific notices before sharing sensitive data.

4. Google and Gmail data

Connecting Gmail is optional. If you authorize the integration, InsightFlow uses the Google permissions displayed during consent for the connected email-sending feature and related sender configuration. The intended use is to send invitation messages initiated by authorized InsightFlow users; it is not intended for reading personal email content, advertising, or selling Google user data.

  • We use Gmail access only for the functionality described above and permitted by the consent you grant.
  • OAuth credentials and tokens are intended to be handled by the server rather than embedded in the client application. Access tokens and refresh tokens must be protected as confidential credentials.
  • You can disconnect the integration from InsightFlow when that option is available and revoke InsightFlow's access through your Google Account permissions.
  • After access is revoked, sending through that connection will stop. Some records may be retained where needed for security, audit, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes.

Google's use of information received through Google APIs is subject to the applicable Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure, authentication, database, hosting, or other service providers that support InsightFlow, and may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect users and the service. We do not authorize service providers to use information for unrelated purposes. We do not sell or share Google user data for advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and required records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable law. Retention periods can vary by data type and organization settings. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of account-related information, contact us using the details below. Some information may need to be retained where legally required or necessary for security and audit purposes.

7. Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your credentials confidential and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Children's privacy

InsightFlow is intended for people who can lawfully use the service in their jurisdiction and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. International processing

Depending on the hosting and service providers used, information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as InsightFlow changes. The “Last updated” date above indicates when the policy was most recently revised. Material changes may also be communicated through the service where appropriate.
