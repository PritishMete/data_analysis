About the application

A clearer workspace for data and teams

InsightFlow brings data-related work and team administration into a shared workspace. Features available to a user depend on their account, assigned role, and organization permissions.

01

Spreadsheet workflows

Work with spreadsheet files and support data-focused tasks in an organized workspace.

02

Team and access management

Help authorized organization users manage employee invitations, team membership, and role-based access.

03

Optional Gmail connection

When an authorized user connects Gmail, InsightFlow can use the approved Gmail permissions to send application invitation emails.

Privacy and permissions

Gmail is an optional integration and requires account authorization. Review our Privacy Policy for information about data handling and our Terms and Conditions for service terms.
